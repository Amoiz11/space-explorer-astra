# Lunar Landing Site and Astrodynamics Disclaimers
Reference: Space Explorer SRS Section 27.3 & Mission Architecture Rules
Status: Mandatory Disclaimers

## 1. Landing Site Planning Support Disclaimer
Candidate sites are planning-support zones; the tool does not certify any site as safe; DEM resolution cannot detect small hazards. Candidate landing sites are evaluation areas for mission planning, not certified landing pads.

## 2. Geometric Line-of-Sight vs. Radio Communication Link
A positive geometric line-of-sight between a lunar surface position and Earth ground stations (DSN) or lunar orbiters does NOT guarantee an established RF radio communication link. Multi-path reflections, antenna pointing loss, terrain diffraction, lunar horizon grazing angles, ionospheric scintillation, and transmitter power budgets significantly affect link closure. Line-of-sight indicates geometric visibility opportunity only.

## 3. Solar Illumination vs. Power Availability
The geometric presence of solar rays (sun elevation > 0 degrees relative to the horizon) does NOT guarantee electrical power generation. Solar array tilt angle, dust accumulation, local horizon topography, shadowing by rover masts or lander structures, low grazing incidence angles, and extreme cryogenic operating temperatures affect solar panel efficiency.

## 4. Lunar DEM and Slope Resolution Limits
Lunar digital elevation models (LOLA DEM and stereo imagery reconstructions) have inherent spatial resolution limitations (e.g. 5m to 30m per pixel). Slopes calculated over these grid cells represent average slope over tens of meters; DEM resolution cannot detect small hazards.
